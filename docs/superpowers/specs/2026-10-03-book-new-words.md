Exclude words that merely reappear in dialogue, proper noun boxes, phonetics, notes, and exercises. Lesson 2 may add 姐姐 (older sister) once, after the printed list.

The lesson pages, reading list, single-word review, flashcards, writing, and lesson vocabulary quizzes all use this exact ordered list. The combined vocabulary bank and combined vocabulary quizzes use the union of these lists, in lesson and book order. Searching and an explicitly requested ten-question quiz sample may show a subset; otherwise the complete book order is preserved. Sentence quizzes and other textbook learning material retain their existing data.

Book page 71 prints 块（元） and 角（毛） as two entries with alternative forms, so each is one review item. Page 72 prints （一）点儿 with an optional prefix, also one item. The individual forms remain available for dialogue matching and speech. The added 姐姐 is labeled as an addition rather than a printed book entry.
